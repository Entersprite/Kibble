# KibbleMac maintainer entry point

Follow the application checkout's root `AGENTS.md` to locate KibbleDocs.
Before editing this module, read that private checkout's
`docs/design/current-architecture-and-rules.md` and the **KibbleMac** section
(`#kibblemac`) of `docs/design/module-architecture-guide.md`, including its
pre-edit and completion checklist. Resolve these paths from KibbleDocs,
not from this module. Keep detailed maintainer rules in the private checkout.
